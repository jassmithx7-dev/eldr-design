# Pantheon

*The divine layer of Eldr. Vela and Forn are the primary powers. Six lesser gods operate beneath them — not subordinate, but lesser in scope. Their allegiances are not loyalties: they are interests.*

---

## Vela and Forn

The dominant conflict. Vela is the rising goddess of domination. Forn is the forgotten god of good, constrained and masked — present in the world as Arn without knowing what he is.

---

## The Six Lesser Gods

The lesser gods do not take sides for reasons of love or duty. They take sides — or refuse to — because of what Vela's victory would cost their domain specifically. The path to turning neutrals is not argument from principle. It is showing them exactly what they lose if she wins.

### Aligned with Forn

**Tyra of the Drawn Breath** — Healing, home, community.
The most beloved of the six. The most directly threatened by the Long Fade. Her domain is the fabric of ordinary life — what war erodes and what Vela's dominion would hollow out permanently. She is with Forn not out of theology but because Vela's world has no room for what Tyra is.

**Skorra of the Everwood** — Nature, forest, survival.
With Forn, but on her own terms. She is not anyone's soldier. The Everwood does not take commands. She opposes Vela because dominion requires domestication — a world under one will is a world with its wild places managed, bounded, used. Skorra will not negotiate that.

---

### Neutral — Each Has a Price

**Valdrek the Unbroken** — War, honor.
Opposes Vela philosophically, but not as an ally of Forn. His objection is domain-specific: dominion ends war. A world under Vela's total control is a world without conflict, without the test of arms, without the thing that makes Valdrek's domain real. He will not be commanded by Forn. He needs a specific argument on his own terms — not "help us," but "here is what her victory means for war itself."

**Mordrek of the Final Dark** — Death, passage.
Opposes soul-trapping specifically. His domain is passage — the transit from life to whatever follows. Vela traps souls, bends the dead to her purpose, refuses the passage. That is the one thing Mordrek will not tolerate. On everything else, death comes regardless of who wins. His neutrality has a single crack: show him that she is stealing what is his.

**Morrven of the Deep** — Sea, precise debts.
Runs on contracts. Not loyalty, not philosophy — exact agreements, honored exactly. She will side with whoever makes the better deal and holds to its terms without deviation. The Golden Wake is already courting her. She is the one lesser god whose allegiance can be purchased if the terms are right.

---

### Compromised

**Theldorn the Mindwarden** — Wisdom, secrets, prophecy.
Sold information to Vela. Owes her something — the nature of the debt is not yet known. He cannot act against her until it is resolved. His domain includes prophecy, but this is an attribute of the god, not a narrative device aimed at the protagonist. Nothing Theldorn knows will be handed to the player as destiny.

## Divine Mechanics (Narrative, Not System)

The gods are not resources. They are political actors with interests, debts, and limits. Getting scattered powers to choose to stand together is the shape of the mid-game divine layer — and it works only if each act of alignment costs something real and is earned on the god's own terms.
