# Glossary

*Every proper noun used in the vault is defined here, once, on first use. Spelling and meaning in this document are authoritative. Any note that uses a term not listed here must add it before the note can be promoted to reviewed.*

---

**Eldr** — The name of the world. Also the project title. Do not explain or etymologize the name in-fiction. First appeared in: [[design-pillars]].

**Eld** — The ambient magical force present everywhere in the world. The in-world term for what functions mechanically as mana. Perceived only by a sensitive minority; worked with only by those who have also studied. First appeared in: [[world-rules]].

**Ashfall Keep** — The frontier stronghold that anchors the vertical slice. Half-ruined, half-defended; last line before the blighted advance. Contains the Falling Gate and the old shrine beneath. First appeared in: [[game-premise]].

**Falling Gate** — The outer gate / crisis zone of Ashfall Keep; the player's first battlefield and evacuation choke point. First appeared in: [[zone-the-falling-gate]].

**Arn** — The player's oldest friend; Act 1 companion. A real mortal soul; never aware that Forn is under his life. He was never a god. At the end of Act 1 his soul passes to Lumen and Forn holds the body. After the credits Forn leaves and Arn comes back as himself. He does not remember being a god. That return is given, not earned, and it is Forn leaving, not a spell. Do not hint at it in Act 1. First appeared in: [[game-premise]].

**Forn** — God of good. Vela hid him inside Arn, a real man who does not know, to weaken him, because Forn has beaten her on even terms and she cannot kill him that way. All the Eld in the world, used against that weakened god, would kill him. If Forn dies, nothing takes his place. At the end of Act 1 he holds Arn's body. After the credits he leaves the body and ascends. Not a party upgrade. Do not hint at the ascension in Act 1. First appeared in: [[pantheon]].

**Lumen** — The rest-place of Eldr; where mortal souls go when passage completes. Arn's soul passes here at the end of Act 1 when Forn takes the body. Magic cannot raise the dead. Arn's later return is Forn leaving, not a spell. Do not hint at it in Act 1. First appeared in: [[game-premise]].

**Settled Host** — Vela's living frontier army at Ashfall Keep. Humanoid; subject to morale. First appeared in: [[enemy-roster-ashfall]].

**the Bound** — Souls denied passage and used as weapons; hollowed / caged. No morale. First appeared in: [[enemy-roster-ashfall]].

**Soulwright** — Act 1 end boss under Ashfall Keep. Large solo. Ritual specialist who built and runs the soul-trap. Not Vela; intermediary champion. First appeared in: [[enemy-roster-ashfall]].

**Gatebreaker** — Living siege warbeast used by the Settled Host at Ashfall's gate. Large; no morale. First appeared in: [[enemy-roster-ashfall]].

**Voryn** — Eld-Weaver; party member Acts 1–3. Ascends at the game's ending. Has a prior history with the Pale Circle that he went too far in. First appeared in: [[design-pillars]].

**Drift** — The accumulation of pole-aligned Eld choices, tracked as an internal integer [−30, +30]. Not a morality score or visible meter — the player reads their position from the world's behavior. Applies to all classes. Full definition: [[eld-magic]]. First appeared in: [[eld-magic]].

**Vela** — Goddess of domination. She wants to rule Eldr. Patient, incorporating; works through leverage rather than force. She hid Forn inside Arn to weaken him, and means to use all the Eld in the world against that weakened god. She believes that kill would make her unable to die. She is wrong. If she is destroyed, someone must take her place. Full entry: [[pantheon]]. First appeared in: [[game-premise]].

**Theldorn the Mindwarden** — Lesser god of wisdom, secrets, and prophecy. Enthralled with Vela. Sold her the ancient knowledge that enabled Forn's mortal suppression — willingly, for desire. Compromised; cannot act against her. Full entry: [[pantheon]]. First appeared in: [[pantheon]].

**Mordrek of the Final Dark** — Lesser god of death and passage. Neutral; opposes soul-trapping specifically because it steals what is his domain. His neutrality has one crack: evidence that Vela is stealing souls rather than allowing passage. Full entry: [[pantheon]]. First appeared in: [[pantheon]].

**Varnstead** — The civilian settlement adjacent to Ashfall Keep; the population being evacuated through the Falling Gate at the start of the vertical slice. First appeared in: [[opening-scene-outline]].

**Ravn** — The protagonist's canon default name. Human male, 30s+, veteran resistance leader. He is what becomes now: the present runs through him because he is the one still standing and he acts. He does not become a god. He is not the next coming. No prophecy, no bloodline, no hidden power waking up. Do not define him by the phrase "he is not chosen." Stakes stay the people in front of him. He leads; Arn stands beside him as a peer because Arn chooses to. Player-editable at character creation; companions address him as "Captain" in VO. First appeared in: [[protagonist]].

**Bram** — Goliath; Blade (Fighter). Party member Acts 1–3. Friend of the player. First appeared in: [[design-pillars]].

**Fen** — Halfling; Shadow (Thief). Party member Acts 1–3. Friend of the player; closest to Dag. First appeared in: [[design-pillars]].

**Anchor** — The sealed vessel holding Forn's severed divine self-knowledge; the mechanism that sustains his mortal suppression beneath Arn. Maintained by the Soulwright's soul-trap ritual beneath Ashfall Keep. Shatters when Forn emerges at the Act 1 climax. Not loot; not player-interactable. Full entry: [[relic-the-anchor]]. First appeared in: [[relic-the-anchor]].
