## The Line

*(Name and structure decided 2026-10-10.)*

- **What it is:** The organized army resisting Vela. One army with a command structure and ranks — not a loose alliance of holdouts.
- **The protagonist's place:** A Captain of the Line, leading one company that has been in arms together for about ten years. See [[protagonist]].
- **History:** The open war has run 15 to 20 years ([[timeline]]). The story begins with the Line already losing ground ([[game-premise]]).
- **Relationships:** Enemy of the Settled Host. Allied with Ironhold and the Mountain-Hold, who keep their own oath and are not part of the Line.
- **Vertical-slice relevance:** The Line holds the Falling Gate at Ashfall Keep while Varnstead evacuates. The protagonist is the ranking officer at the keep; no superior is present.

## The Settled Host

- **What it is:** Vela's living frontier army. People who chose, or were pressured into, her settlement. Humanoid; they break, shout and flee. See [[enemy-roster-ashfall]].
- **Works alongside:** the Bound — souls denied passage and used as weapons. An enemy family, not a faction with its own will.
- **Relationships:** Enemy of the Line.
- **Vertical-slice relevance:** The besieging force at Ashfall Keep.

## The Mountain-Hold and Ironhold

- **What it is:** The oath-bound Goliath compact of the northern passes, and the hold it is allied to. Defined in [[goliath]].
- **Relationships:** Ally of the Line with its own obligations. Not Ashfall Keep, and not part of the Line's chain of command.
- **Vertical-slice relevance:** Bram arrives late to the Falling Gate from the Ironhold line ([[companion-bram]]).

## The Pale Circle

- **What it is:** The institution that categorizes and teaches Eld practice ([[eld-magic]]). Voryn trained there and left after going too far into Vela's pole ([[companion-voryn]]).
- **Vertical-slice relevance:** None on stage. Background to Voryn.

## The Golden Wake

- Named once, in [[pantheon]], as courting Morrven. Nothing else is defined.
