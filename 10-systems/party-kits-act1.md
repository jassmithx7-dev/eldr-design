## Arn — Paladin of Forn

Arn is outside the Eld system: no Eld pool, no Drift ([[eld-magic]], [[companion-arn]]). His healing is answered prayer to Forn, the same weak answer Forn's other faithful get ([[game-premise]]). Mechanically his limit is **prayers per fight** instead of Eld.

**Placeholder stats (level 2, TUNE):** HP 22 · Medium armor + shield (3) · Longsword 5 + STR 1.

| Skill | Cost | Weight | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mend** | Prayer (3 per fight) | Normal | Heal one conscious ally for 8 HP. |
| **Raise Up** | Prayer (1 per fight) | Heavy | Revive a Downed ally at 40% HP. |
| **Stand Between** | — | Quick | Guard one ally until Arn's next turn: hits meant for them hit Arn instead, and he takes 2 less from each. Fills bond when it triggers. |
| **Shield Bash** | — | Normal, **Interrupt** | 4 damage. Interrupts a telegraphed move. |
| **Hold Fast** | Prayer (1 per fight) | Heavy | Until Arn's next turn, the whole active party takes 2 less from each hit. |

**The decisions his kit creates:** heal now or save a prayer for a revive; guard the soft target or stop the telegraph; take the slow party-wide guard before a big hit lands.

**What his kit teaches the slice:** Shield Bash is the party's most reliable interrupt in the early fights, before the Binder Acolytes and the Soulwright test it. Losing him at the end of Act 1 removes the party's healer and best interrupt at once; Solen covers healing in Act 2 by different means.

### Presentation rules for Arn

- His prayers look like **any paladin of Forn's**: a short, warm, plain light at the hands and on the healed ally. Never brighter, larger or stranger than other faithful get. No halo, no uncanny glow, no survival tell ([[companion-arn]], [[game-premise]]).
- The warm amber matches the Forn-pole Eld colour. That's correct — he openly serves Forn — and it is not a hint.
- His animations should read as a soldier first: shield work, short prayers between blows, not ceremony.

## Bram, Fen, Voryn

- **Bram:** basic attack + the four Fighter Eld abilities (Bulwark, Stand Firm, Break, Execute). Front row. Break is his interrupt.
- **Fen:** basic attack + the four Thief Eld abilities (Fade, Mark, Expose, Exploit). Exploit is his interrupt. Fen's conscious Eld sensitivity is characterisation only — it gives no combat bonus ([[companion-fen]]).
- **Voryn:** basic attack + a slice spell list (open). Back row. Counterspell is his interrupt.

Each also has the item-ability slot.
