# Fight Scene

[[combat-core]] is the older grid writeup. That note is the previous combat. This note is how a fight looks now. The old file stays. Class numbers stay in it.

When an enemy engages, walking stops. The same room stays on screen. A menu comes up. A list shows who acts next. The player picks a weapon, a spell, an ability, or an item. Quick attacks happen when picked. A heavy spell or ability can be seen coming, and a hit can break it before it lands. When the fight ends, the menu goes away and the story continues in that same room.

No squares. No flanking puzzles. No real-time wandering. No wipe to a battle stage. Fights stay short. The story is the game. It is not a war game, and it is not a separate arena.

Classes are what that menu is best at. Armor means the person is still standing when the menu returns. Race is one locked trick on a companion’s body, not a picker, and it does not need to be designed in this note.

The decision is whether to pick something that happens now, or to put a heavy spell on the list and wait while other people act.

## Cast time

Magic is not all instant. A quick spell happens when you pick it. A heavy spell does not. You choose it on your turn, it shows on the list of who acts next, and it fires when it reaches the front. A hit on the caster before that can break it.

This is not a clock counting seconds, and not a bar that fills in real time. The wait is other people taking their turns.

Attack math, armor numbers, and spell costs are not restated here. They stay in [[combat-core]] and [[eld-magic]].
