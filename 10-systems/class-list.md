# Class List

*Refocus for the slice. The yard already picks the class. This note says what those three are, and what a fight is. It does not replace [[combat-core]].*

## The list

Three classes. No fourth. The weapon table picks the class only.

| Table | Class | In a fight |
|-------|--------|------------|
| Sword | **Fighter** | Stands where the line needs a body. Hits in melee. Small Eld pool. Spends it to cover someone, or to finish someone already hurt. |
| Knife | **Thief** | Moves to the side of the trouble. Makes the company's next hit easier. Small Eld pool. Spends it to get clear, or to show where to hit. |
| Staff | **Caster** | Casts. Large Eld pool. On a good round, matches a fighter. Does not clear a group alone. |

The names on the companions are those people. Bram is a Blade. Fen is a Shadow. Voryn is an Eld-Weaver. Arn is not an Eld caster. Solen is later. None of those are a class Ravn chooses.

Ravn takes one weapon off one table. The game says the class in plain words and stops. Hold the Gate comes after that.

The choices inside a class are the talent trees in [[class-progression]]. Three trees per class, the same names as the playstyles. From level 2, one point per level, spendable in any of that class's trees. Not a second question at the table. The game suggests one tree. A free change sits at the first camp.

| Class | Trees |
|-------|--------|
| Fighter | Guardian, Champion, Warlord |
| Thief | Thief, Assassin, Shadowdancer |
| Caster | Controller, Evoker, Abjurer |

Level 1 is only the basic hit (or Eld Bolt for a caster). The first real button is the first point. A slice character has 4 points. A capstone takes 6 points in one tree, so it is not in the slice.

## How a fight works

Combat stays a turn-based grid. That is the Baldur's Gate line this game already picked: a separate tactical map, where standing still or stepping aside is the decision. Not a real-time brawl.

On a turn, a person can move, do one main thing, and one smaller thing.

- The main thing is a hit, an Eld act, a spell, or helping someone who is down.
- The smaller thing is a potion, extra steps, or one shout when someone nearby goes down.

Three questions, every turn. Where do I stand. What do I spend the turn on. Who do I hit.

The company shows up in two small rules already in [[combat-core]]. If someone else hit that enemy last round, your hit is a little easier. If someone goes down near you, you can shout once to help them stay up. That is the twenty years. It is not a combo meter.

Hold the Gate is also about getting people out. Clearing the enemies is not the only win. That stay locked on the quest.

Eld does not come back during the fight. It comes back at rest. Fighter and Thief spend a flat pool of 6. The Caster's pool is the large one. Which acts you spend it on, cover or finish, is the drift. That happens in the fight. It is not a second choice at the table.

## What this pass leaves alone

The numbers, the grid, initiative, morale, and the downed state stay in [[combat-core]]. This note does not retune them.

Still open, and not decided here: the stat array, level-up choices inside a class, the full spell list, knockback numbers, and how a wipe is counted beyond what combat-core already says (back to the last rest, no permadeath, death is a story event).

The trees are already written. This note does not replace them. The number to watch for "powerful, not ridiculous" is Evoker's Fireball: 3d6 to everyone in the blast. A Settled Levy has 20 HP. One of those casts can erase a group. The other Fireball, in the spell list, is 2d6 to one person. Pick one before calling the caster tuned.
