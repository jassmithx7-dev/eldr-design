# World Presentation

On 2026-10-10 the human locked this. It is the way the game is built unless he reopens it.

The world is 2D pixel art with height and light, in the manner of Sea of Stars as construction only. Grim, not bright. People are pictures, not rigged 3D characters. The camera stays at one high angle. Floors can have steps and walls you can see the face of. A fight stays in that same picture. Quick actions happen when picked. A heavy spell or ability waits on the turn list, and a hit can break it before it lands. Most spells share a few poses (idle, walk, quick blow, heavy cast, hit, down). What makes a spell different is the rule and a small effect on the picture, not a unique animation.

Do not propose full 3D people or full 3D rooms unless the human reopens this.
