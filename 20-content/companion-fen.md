# Fen

*The one who decided before anyone asked him to.*

## Personality

**Spine:** Tasselhoff energy with a war-adult twist — bright, curious, loyal chatter — until Eld-wrongness hits, and his **spider-sense** fires (instinct alert, not mute, not a power menu).

**Temperament:**
- Frequent talker on the march and in downtime: questions, observations, fills silence (adult, not cartoon kender bit).
- Curiosity and friendliness first; he reads people accurately and still chooses warmth.
- **Tell (EldSense):** When ambient Eld wrongness spikes, Fen goes **alert** — stops mid-joke, scans, hand toward weapon. Short instinct bark only ("Wait." / "Something's wrong."). No Eld lecture. No speech about destiny.
- **Ashfall triggers (locked):** (1) contaminated gatehouse shrine (opening); (2) Soulwright rite starting (Phase 1→2). Not every Binder cast; not Unpassed-first as a required beat.
- **Game budget (locked):** **3–4 EldSense beats total** across the whole game. Ashfall spends **2**. Remaining **1–2** reserved for Acts 2–3 (scenes TBD). Never ambient spam / every wrong room.
- **Display (locked, override 2026-07-27):** Body language (pose) + instinct bark + party **portrait pulse**. Not a mute chip (`Fen — still` retired). Flag: `Fen_EldSense` — see [[slice-ashfall-unity-map]].
- Not comic-relief clown; lightness is real personality under a losing war, not obliviousness.

**What he wants at Ashfall:**
- Keep *his* people — protagonist, Dag, the company — moving and alive through the gate.
- Stay useful (scouting, paths, watching faces) without turning into a sticky-fingers gag reel.
- Not abandon anyone he has already claimed.

**How he treats people:**
- **Protagonist:** Chose them first; loyalty never revised down. If an order would harm people he cares about, he **sidesteps** — softens or redirects the harm rather than open mutiny or cold compliance.
- **Dag:** Equal weird duo. Banter both ways; neither is “in charge.” Locked pair — player gets both or neither. When EldSense fires, Dag’s grumble stops or sharpens — he reads the alert.
- **Arn:** Easy trust; looks to Arn’s steadiness when things get heavy; EldSense may fire near Arn if the *field* is wrong — never as a tell that Arn is holy.
- **Bram:** Friendly; climbs the mountain of Bram’s size with talk, not awe. Bram notices when Fen goes coiled/alert.
- **Voryn:** Keeps talking at the wall. Does not pry about the hand. Notices when Voryn goes colder; EldSense may fire if Eld-wrong and Voryn’s craft overlap in the same room.

**Hard edges (will / won't):**
- **Will:** Claim people early and keep them. Fill space so the company doesn’t drown in dread. Soften a bad order without a speech about betrayal. Snap to instinct alert when the field goes wrong.
- **Won't:** Constant pickpocket / “borrowed this” comedy. Perform fearlessness as a joke about danger. Explain his Eld-sense as special destiny. Go mute-as-identity (silence is not the tell).
- **Never:** Turn EldSense into a divine hint about Arn/Forn. The sense is about the *field*, not about Arn being holy.

**Class note:** Shadow (thief) kit stays for combat/infiltration. Personality **downplays** theft-as-identity — loyalty, chatter, and EldSense matter more than stealing bits.

**Slice job:** Make the company feel alive and bonded; use EldSense as readable pressure at the gatehouse shrine and Soulwright rite start — without spoiling Arn.

## Role in the Story

Fen has been with the protagonist for about ten years. He arrived with Dag. He stays because he has never considered leaving.

## Character Hook

Fen decided the protagonist was his before the protagonist had a chance to decide anything. No fear, no filter, no conditions. The loyalty arrived before it was earned and has never been revised downward.

This is not naivety — he reads people; he is not easily fooled. He simply made up his mind and that was that.

## Eld-Sensitivity

Fen has standard binary Eld-sensitivity. He is not a caster and has never studied. Because he has no formal training, he has never learned to tune out ambient Eld. He perceives disturbances in the field raw and unfiltered — before trained practitioners who have built habits of selective attention. He registers wrongness before he can name it. He does not consider this remarkable.

**Personality link:** The sensitivity surfaces as **EldSense behavior** — alert pose, instinct bark, portrait pulse — not as a magic button or plot decoder. Characterization + thin HUD. Not a combat ability. Not a quest trigger that names Forn.

## Locked Pair with Dag

Fen and Dag come as a unit. The player gets both or neither. **Dynamic (locked):** equal weird duo — banter both ways; neither runs the other. The history is not explained. It does not need to be.

## Slice Presence

Fen is present in the vertical slice as a party member with established history. He is not the slice's focal companion — that remains Arn. His character note here establishes the foundation; his full arc belongs to the complete game.
