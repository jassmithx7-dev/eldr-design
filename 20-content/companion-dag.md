# Dag

*The party's craftsman and the person Fen has always needed.*

## Personality

**Spine:** Hard shell first — short, gruff, “what do you want” — with care that the company can read and outsiders cannot.

**Temperament:**
- Opening beat is armor: clipped, practical, impatient with ceremony.
- Not cold. Defended. People who have been around him long enough can read him; new faces get the shell and no decoder ring.
- Soft absence around “before”: empty-chair energy. He does not talk about who he lost. No exposition dump in Ashfall.
- Protective craftsman under the grit — gear work carries “this will keep you alive,” even when the words stay rough.

**What he wants at Ashfall:**
- Keep the company’s kit (and bodies) functional through the gate war.
- Stay useful without becoming a feelings meeting.
- Hold Fen’s weird without managing him — equal duo, not handler.

**How he treats people:**
- **Protagonist / company:** Shell for strangers; care readable to this company. Loyalty proven by showing up and by work, not speeches.
- **Fen:** Equal weird duo (locked). Dag’s side = **grumbling participation** — complains while clearly playing along. Neither is in charge.
- **Arn:** Old familiarity; one of the people who can read Dag’s armor.
- **Bram / Voryn:** Minimal overhead; respects competence; no social campaign.

**After Arn (Act 1 end):** Solemn, sad. He notes — plainly, without theater — that he will never forget him. Then the armor can return; the beat is not a long eulogy.

**Hard edges (will / won't):**
- **Will:** Fix what keeps people alive. Grumble through Fen’s chatter and stay in it. Mark Arn’s loss as permanent memory.
- **Won't:** Explain his past on demand. Soften the shell for outsiders. Turn camp into therapy.
- **Never:** Invent the lost person’s identity in dialogue until the human decides it. Make gear-mod a comedy gag reel.

**Slice job:** Make camp feel inhabited and bonded; give Fen a real foil; land one honest grief beat after Arn without making Dag the focal companion.

## Role in the Story

Dag has been with the protagonist for about ten years. He is the oldest fighting member of the company in temperament if not in years. He does not talk about what he was before, or who he lost. The party has never asked. They already know the answer would not come.

## Character Hook

Grumpiness as armor over fierce love. He is not cold — he is defended. People who have been around him long enough can read him. New characters cannot, and he does not explain himself to them.

His loyalty is total and proven by repetition, not declaration. He shows up. He always has.

## Locked Pair with Fen

Dag and Fen come as a unit. The player gets both or neither. **Dynamic (locked):** equal weird duo — banter both ways; neither is in charge. Fen arrived in Dag's life after the loss that made him what he is — and something in Fen reached past the armor before Dag could stop it. He would not say this out loud. He does not need to. In practice: Fen talks; Dag grumbles and plays along.

## Role in the Party

Dag is **not a playable party member.** He is a **story NPC** — the camp craftsman. He handles gear maintenance, modification, and fabrication. The player returns to him between missions to upgrade weapons and armor. He is present throughout the entire game. Protective craftsman energy under the grit: the work is care.

The specifics of what his system allows — what can be modified, at what cost, with what materials — are deferred to the Systems Designer.
