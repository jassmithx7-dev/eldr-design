# Dag

*The party's craftsman and the person Fen has always needed.*

## Role in the Story

Dag has been with the protagonist for approximately twenty years. He is the oldest fighting member of the company in temperament if not in years. He does not talk about what he was before, or who he lost. The party has never asked. They already know the answer would not come.

## Character Hook

Grumpiness as armor over fierce love. He is not cold — he is defended. People who have been around him long enough can read him. New characters cannot, and he does not explain himself to them.

His loyalty is total and proven by repetition, not declaration. He shows up. He always has.

## Locked Pair with Fen

Dag and Fen come as a unit. The player gets both or neither. Fen arrived in Dag's life after the loss that made him what he is — and something in Fen reached past the armor before Dag could stop it. He would not say this out loud. He does not need to.

## Role in the Party

Dag is **not a playable party member.** He is a **story NPC** — the camp craftsman. He handles gear maintenance, modification, and fabrication. The player returns to him between missions to upgrade weapons and armor. He is present throughout the entire game.

The specifics of what his system allows — what can be modified, at what cost, with what materials — are deferred to the Systems Designer.
