# The Falling Gate

*The first vertical-slice zone — the outer gate of Ashfall Keep.*

## Overview

The Falling Gate is the outer gate of **Ashfall Keep**, a frontier stronghold on the edge of the blighted lands. The keep is half-ruined, half-defended, and already showing the strain of the war. Vela's forces have pushed close enough that this gate is now the last line of defense between the living settlements and the advancing darkness.

## Purpose in the Story

This zone is where the player first experiences the scale of the resistance's exhaustion. The settlement is not glamorous; it is battered, frightened, and running out of time. The player must keep the gate standing long enough for civilians to evacuate and for the party to reach the old shrine beneath the keep.

## Key Features

- A collapsing outer gatehouse with limited defensive positions.
- A chapel or shrine that has become a site of strange power.
- Narrow interior routes that make the battle feel urgent and spatially constrained.
- A final descent into the old undercroft where the party confronts the first true sign of Vela's influence.

## Encounter Intent

The zone should feel like a desperate holding action. The enemies are not just monsters; they are the pressure of a world already being overwritten. Enemy types for this zone: [[enemy-roster-ashfall]].
