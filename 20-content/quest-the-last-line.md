# The Last Line

*The vertical-slice questline.*

## Premise

The resistance has one final chance to hold the Falling Gate at Ashfall Keep long enough for the civilians to escape. The player must lead the defense, secure the interior routes, and descend into the old shrine beneath the keep to stop the corruption from spreading.

## Quest Structure

1. **Hold the Gate**  
   The party fights to keep the outer defenses from collapsing while civilians evacuate from **Varnstead**.

   **Completion (locked — hybrid):** both must be true:
   - **Gate wave clear** — the authored Hold-the-Gate encounter(s) are won (enemies eliminated / routed per [[combat-core]]).
   - **Evac meter full** — enough civilians reach the safe staging / exit count (Quest Machine counter; tune in playtest).

   Either alone does **not** advance. Clearing waves without evac = you held ground but people didn't get out. Evac without a real hold = soft-fail of combat proof. When both complete → objective **Reach the Shrine** activates; keep interior opens.

2. **Reach the Shrine**  
   The player pushes through the keep and learns that Vela's influence is not only military; it is ritual and spiritual. The shrine beneath the gate has been used to maintain the [[relic-the-anchor]] — a sealed vessel that is the true mechanism of Forn's thirty-year suppression. The shrine is not a discovery to be solved; it is evidence of something the player does not yet have the context to read.

3. **Confront the Source**  
   **End of Act 1 — the Soulwright.** Soul-trap: **Bram** and **Fen** caught (false wipe). **Voryn stays up and fighting** with player + Arn. Arn's stress breaks Forn's suppression; Forn shatters the trap once; Bram and Fen return. **Arn speaks a final conversation with the player**, then his soul passes and Forn holds the body. Full beat order locked in [[game-premise]]; boss kit in [[enemy-roster-ashfall]].

## Emotional Function

This quest should feel like a final stand that turns into sudden personal loss. The player should not understand Arn/Forn until the climax lands. No slow divine drip earlier in the quest.

### Stub 1 — Fen ↔ Dag camp (grumble-play)
**When:** The first rest after Hold the Gate is complete (gate wave clear and evac meter full), before the keep push. The keep's back room. No separate zone note.  
**What:** One linear scene. Fen fills the quiet. Dag complains and plays along at the bench. Arn takes Ravn aside for one unfinished worry, then stops. Stub 2 plays in this same downtime, after Arn.  
**Job:** Make the rest feel inhabited before the keep gets worse. The player can stand, listen, and watch Dag finish one small piece of work.

#### Dag's fire

**What the player is watching:** The gate is over. In the back room, the company is sitting down. Fen is the one still on his feet, talking about a strap on Ravn's kit that squeaked the whole way in. Dag is already fixing that strap, and he makes Fen sit and hold it. Arn starts to say Fen has not come off the gate, sees him sit, and stops. At the table, Bram has already set a bowl for Voryn. Voryn treats that as the fact that he was included.

The room: a fire, Dag's bench, bedrolls, the wounded along one wall, a table someone eats at. Crowded. The war is off. A back room, not a tavern and not a war room. Ravn stands in it. Nothing asks him to choose.

<!-- ASSUMPTION: The one worry Arn does not finish is Fen still up from the gate. Civilians still moving is not this rest. -->

Fen is by the bench, still talking.

> **Fen:** "Ravn. Your strap sang the whole way off the gate. I'm still hearing it."

Dag has that strap on the bench. He does not look up. The complaint is him staying in it.

> **Dag:** "Then sit down and hold it. It's leather. It isn't a story."

Fen sits. He takes the loose end.

> **Fen:** "I'm sitting. I've got the end."

Dag sets one rivet. The strap goes quiet. He does not say it will hold.

> **Dag:** "Hold still. There. Now leave it. Ravn heard it already."

Arn is a step off, close enough that only Ravn is meant to hear. He was going to say more. Fen is in the seat, the strap in his hands.

> **Arn:** "Ravn. He was still on the gate."

Arn stops. He does not add a plan.

Bram is at the table. A bowl is already at the empty end. This is Stub 2, the same sitting-down.

> **Bram:** "Voryn. I put yours down when I sat. End of the table."

> **Voryn:** "Then it was never a question."

Voryn sits. He does not move the bowl, and he does not thank Bram. That is the last line. Dag is still at the bench. The room keeps going. No one asks Ravn what comes next.

No fight. No Charisma gate. No dialogue menu. No EldSense. Companions say Ravn. Fen is he.

### Stub 2 — Bram includes Voryn
**When:** The same downtime as Stub 1, after Arn stops. Not a separate corridor scene.  
**What:** Bram treats Voryn as already in — the bowl was set when Bram sat. Voryn's return is the one dry line in Dag's fire, and then he sits. No fix campaign. No nod staged on top of the line.  
**Job:** Plant inclusion before the Soulwright three-up (player + Arn + Voryn).

**Job:** Decision friction that feels adult.
