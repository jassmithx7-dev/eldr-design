## What holds

**The war prologue has no gods and no Eld.** The title is the word Eldr. It is not explained. The screen says the war has gone against the free lands, Vela's armies are taking ground, Varnstead has broken, and the people are falling back on Ashfall. Ravn is already there. The gate is still open. Vela is not called a god on that screen. Eld is not said.

**Ravn leads. Arn stands beside him. The scenes say Ravn, not Captain. Fen is he.** In the yard Ravn gives the order. Arn is with the person who cannot run, and he looks to Ravn. From the gatehouse on, Arn stands with him by choice. On the stair Arn walks beside him. At the end he is still beside him. Every companion line in these scenes says Ravn. Fen is he in all of them. No scene calls Ravn Captain.

**The stuck-family fork is remembered at the second fire.** If the adult who can still work got out, that adult is at the fire, the children who walked are with them, and they put one ordinary thing in Ravn's hand. If Ravn dropped the bar, their place is empty, nothing is handed over, and Arn says their sister is already through with the carts and will ask for them. The same one fact is the only one kept for the Act 2 camp: that person is there selling ordinary supplies, or that seller is not there. Dag still does the real repairs either way.

**The walk down still expects both of them to come back up.** Ravn says "Same stair back up." Arn says "On your left." He had asked to leave someone with Dag. Ravn said no. Arn comes, and he does not say it again.

**Arn's goodbye does not hint that he returns after the credits.** He tells Ravn to get them up the stair. Then, for Ravn only: "On your left." Then he is gone. The body stays on its feet. No one says he will come back. No one names what happens after the credits.

**The unanswered line does exist, on the first-fire draft.** At Dag's fire (PR 5) Arn says, and does not finish: "Ravn. Fen still hasn't settled down." The goodbye repeats those words and says no one answers. The goodbye writer was right that this sitting is not in that branch's earlier scenes. PR 14's Stub 1 is still the short placement note. It does not contain the line.

**Dag at the fire does not break the quiet after.** The way down leaves him at the bench: "Go on. I'll keep the fire." The goodbye keeps him there. He is not in the room. The quiet is Fen on his feet, and Voryn and Bram in the room, not taking the moment. Nobody answers the line. Dag is the one who sits Fen down, and he is upstairs, so he cannot answer it here. His absence is why the room stays quiet. It does not poke a hole in it. His grief is still the later camp, which is only a stub.

**The spoken lines stay grim and adult.** No chosen one. No one in the scenes names the friendship. People talk about the cart, the strap, the bar, the stair, and who is stuck.

## What does not connect

1. **The chapel, the first fire, and the second fire are not in one order.** The gatehouse is written as the next thing after the fight: the fight is over, and they walk the passage into the chapel. The first fire is also the first rest after that fight, in the keep's back room, with the war off and no EldSense. The second fire is a return to that same room after the lower court, and it does not replace the first sitting. Fen tells Dag that Ravn would be back. The opening outline still puts the chapel in the opening, with Bram's arrival in the fight just before it. Those cannot all be the next thing after the fight. Read straight through, they have not sat down yet when the chapel scene walks them in from the fight, and the later fire talks as if they already had.

2. **The second fire gives Ravn a seat the first fire never gave him.** At the first fire Ravn stands. Fen sits and holds the strap. Dag works the bench. At the second fire Fen says Dag kept Ravn's end of the bench. Nothing earlier saved him that place.

3. **The yard sends Fen out, and the later scenes have him inside.** Ravn says to get the cart through, Fen goes with it, then they close the gate. The next drafted scenes — the fire, the chapel, the court, the stair — all have Fen with the company. Nothing brings him back in before the gate shuts.

4. **Fen does sit, and the goodbye says he has not.** At the first fire Arn stops because Fen is in the seat. At the second fire Fen sits again. The words were never answered. That part matches. The goodbye also says Fen is on his feet and has not sat down, and that the line is still open as if the sitting never happened. The not-sitting is only true after the trap, and the notes do not say that.

5. **The sister is only a line.** The room has two adults and children. It does not mention a sister. She appears only if the bar drops: already through with the carts, and she will ask. The yard has carts. It does not have this family. Barring the door seals them in with the levy. The later note treats that as the adult not living. The court scene never shows that death.

6. **The scenes say Ravn. The story-decision note still says Captain.** PR 6 and PR 9 have companions say Ravn, and the name is not chosen at the start. PR 7's protagonist note and glossary still say the name is editable and that voiced lines say Captain. The party still "calls him Captain" in that draft. The acted scenes and that decision do not match.

7. **The notes do not say the breath Arn is gone, and they do not say whether Forn speaks.** Premise, Arn's note, the pantheon note, and the Ashfall roster on the story-decision draft lock the order only: the trap breaks, Arn speaks one private line, the soul passes, Forn holds the body. They do not lock the breath, and they do not say if Forn has a line. The goodbye used the smallest version and flagged it: the private line is the last thing Arn says, the body stays up, Forn does not speak, no one names him. That flag is accurate. It is still an assumption.

8. **The goodbye is not the same goodbye in every note.** The scene is two lines. "Get them up the stair" is the order. "On your left" is the private line, the same words as the stair. The story-decision draft says one private line, the same for every class, and not colored by Drift. The top of the quest note on the goodbye branch still says Arn speaks a final conversation. The premise on main, the roster on main, and the Unity map still want that conversation in three Drift variants. Those are different endings to the same stair.

9. **The opening gear does not match the class notes.** Before any steel, four tables name fighter, rogue, mage, and cleric. The religious book does not name a faith. PR 7, PR 9, and main still have three classes: Fighter, Thief, and Caster. The yard branch's protagonist note still says the last words change with class. The goodbye does not.

10. **The places are not on the zone note.** The scenes use a back room, a gatehouse chapel, a lower court under that chapel, and a stair from the fire down to the old shrine. The Falling Gate note names a gatehouse, a chapel, and a descent. It does not name the court, the back room, or this stair. The scenes already say so.
